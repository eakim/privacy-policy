Privacy Policy

UseCycle · Last Updated: September 11, 2026

UseCycle ("UseCycle", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be shared with third-party services, and the choices available to you when you use the UseCycle mobile application (the "App").

By using UseCycle, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

UseCycle is designed to work primarily on your device. Depending on the features you use and the third-party services included in the App, limited information may be collected.

1.1 Item and App Data

UseCycle stores information needed to provide its core replacement-cycle tracking features, such as:

  • Items added to your replacement list
  • Replacement dates and schedules
  • Item categories and names
  • Replacement status
  • App settings and preferences

This information is stored locally on your device using local storage. UseCycle does not require an account or user registration to use its core features.

1.2 Analytics Information

UseCycle may use Google Analytics and/or Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to improve its functionality.

This may include information such as:

  • App launches
  • Screens or features viewed
  • Interactions with app features
  • General usage information
  • Device and app information used for analytics

Analytics information is processed by the applicable Google services according to their privacy policies.

1.3 Advertising Information

UseCycle may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Depending on the device, configuration, and applicable permissions, AdMob may process information such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Information about advertisements displayed
  • Advertising interactions
  • App usage information related to advertising

Advertising-related data may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's applicable privacy policies and advertising settings.

2. Information We Do Not Collect Through Our Own Services

UseCycle does not require users to create an account, provide an email address, or provide a name to use the App's core functionality.

UseCycle does not operate its own user database or cloud account system for replacement-cycle data.

Data stored locally on your device is not transmitted to us simply because it is stored in the App.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core functionality
  • Store and display your replacement-cycle information locally
  • Understand how the App is used
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Provide advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse
Local data: Your replacement-cycle information is designed to remain on your device unless you explicitly export or otherwise share it using a feature provided by your device or the App.

We do not sell your personal information.

UseCycle does not operate a cloud database for your replacement-cycle data. However, third-party analytics and advertising providers may independently process information collected through their SDKs when those services are enabled in the App.

Such third-party services process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Retention

Replacement-cycle data stored locally in the App remains on your device until you delete it, remove the App, or otherwise clear the App's local data, subject to your device's operating system behavior.

Information collected by third-party analytics or advertising services may be retained according to the policies and retention settings of those services.
