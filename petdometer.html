Privacy Policy

Petdometer · Last Updated: September 11, 2026

Petdometer ("Petdometer", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be shared with third-party services, and the choices available to you when you use the Petdometer mobile application (the "App").

By using Petdometer, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, Petdometer may collect the following types of information.

1.1 Account Information

If you create or sign in to a Petdometer account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • User ID or account identifier
  • Authentication-related information

Account authentication and related data may be processed using Supabase.

1.2 Health and Fitness Information

Petdometer uses step-count information to provide its core pet-growth functionality.

Depending on your device, Petdometer may access step-count data through:

  • Apple HealthKit on iOS
  • Android Health Connect on Android

The information may include:

  • Step count
  • Daily step count
  • Related activity or fitness information made available through the applicable health platform

Petdometer only accesses this information after you grant the relevant permission.

Health and fitness information is used to calculate pet growth and provide walking-related features within the App.

We do not use health or fitness information for advertising or advertising-related targeting.

1.3 App Usage Information

We may collect information about how you interact with Petdometer in order to understand app usage and improve the App.

This may include:

  • App launches
  • Screens or features viewed
  • Buttons or features interacted with
  • Missions completed
  • Pet growth events
  • Items used
  • Other interactions with the App

We may use Google Analytics and Firebase services for analytics purposes.

1.4 Advertising Information

Petdometer may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Depending on the device, configuration, and applicable permissions, advertising services may process information such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Information about advertisements displayed
  • Advertising interactions
  • App usage information related to advertising

Advertising-related data may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's applicable privacy policies and advertising settings.

1.5 Game and App Data

Petdometer may store information necessary to provide and synchronize your game progress and app settings, including:

  • Pet type and growth stage
  • Pet growth history
  • Step-related game progress
  • Coins
  • Mission progress and completion
  • Inventory and items
  • Pet or space customization
  • App settings and preferences

If cloud synchronization is enabled, this information may be stored on our servers through Supabase.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts
  • Synchronize your data across devices
  • Provide step-based pet growth features
  • Save and restore game progress
  • Provide missions and rewards
  • Improve app functionality
  • Understand how users interact with the App
  • Monitor and improve app performance
  • Provide advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse
Health and fitness data: Health and fitness information is used to provide the App's walking and pet-growth functionality and is not used for advertising or advertising targeting.
